CampusMail

Find the course emails that need you today. CampusMail sorts a batch of university emails into Needs review, High, Medium and Low, explains every decision, and links each summary to the exact words in the original email. It runs on your own laptop with a free local AI model.

Install on your laptop Source code Sample inbox

Why

Deadlines hide among routine updates, and subject lines don't tell an optional seminar from a required submission. CampusMail reads each email for an action and an explicit deadline. A fixed, published rule set then decides the priority. The AI never decides on its own.

Needs reviewVague or conflicting dates, other time zones, text the app can't verify, or emails that try to instruct the AI. Always shown first.
HighAn action due within 48 hours, an overdue action, or a schedule change for one of your courses within 48 hours.
MediumAny other required action, or an update for one of your courses.
LowGeneral announcements with nothing to do. The word "URGENT" alone never raises a priority.

How it works

ImportValidateExtract with AICheck evidenceRankSummarizeReview
  • Extract: Qwen3 4B, running locally through Ollama, returns structured fields: course, action, deadline, a short summary and exact quotations.
  • Check evidence: code, not the model, verifies that every quotation, deadline, course and number appears in the email. Anything unsupported goes to review.
  • Rank: a deterministic policy in Hong Kong time decides the band, and you can correct any priority or deadline.
  • Export: a plain-text digest, and a calendar file (.ics) with High and Medium deadlines. Items that need review stay out of your calendar.
  • Fallback: if the AI is unavailable, the app says so and shows a clearly labelled rules-only view. It never shows a made-up summary.

Optional extras: a cloud model (OpenAI, Claude, DeepSeek, Qwen) with an explicit confirmation before any email leaves your laptop, and read-only import from Outlook / HKU mail or Gmail.

Set up in about 10 minutes (Windows)

  1. Install Python 3.12 or 3.13 and tick "Add python.exe to PATH".
  2. Install Ollama and leave it running.
  3. Download CampusMail from GitHub (Code → Download ZIP) and unzip it.
  4. Double-click setup.bat. It installs the dependencies and downloads the model (about 2.5 GB, first time only).
  5. Double-click run.bat. Your browser opens CampusMail.
  6. Click Load sample inbox, then Sort and summarize. You should see a High deadline and several review items, each with its source.

On macOS or Linux, use ./setup.sh and ./run.sh. No account, API key or payment is needed.

Limits and responsible use

Check the original email before acting. CampusMail can miss or misread messages, and it does not guarantee that every urgent email is found.
  • It never sends, deletes or changes email, and it never opens links or attachments. Mailbox access is read-only.
  • With the local model, nothing leaves your computer. Nothing is saved or logged, and Clear session forgets everything.
  • English only. Other languages are flagged for review.
  • Development and evaluation use synthetic emails only.
  • AI assistance in building the project is disclosed in the AI use log.
